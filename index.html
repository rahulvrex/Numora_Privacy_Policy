🔒 Privacy Commitment

Privacy Policy

Welcome to Numora Match - Numbers Game. Your privacy and trust are paramount. Numora Match is built with an offline-first architecture designed to provide a rich, relaxing number puzzle experience without tracking your personal identity or compromising your security.

Application: Numora Match - Numbers Game
Package ID: com.aistudio.numoramatch.pqwzkl
Effective Date: September 2026
💾

1. Information We Collect

Numora Match operates primarily on your device. We do not require user account creation, social logins, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords to play.

  • Local Game Progress: Your current stage progression, high scores, total points, completed stages, saved puzzle board state, and custom preferences (sound effects, haptic vibration) are stored locally on your device.
  • Device Diagnostics & Specs: Basic technical device information (such as screen resolution, operating system version, and general hardware configuration) provided automatically by the Android system to ensure smooth grid rendering and prevent application crashes.
Zero Sensitive Permissions: Numora Match requests zero dangerous Android runtime permissions. We do not access your Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, Photos, or External Storage.
⚙️

2. How We Use Information

All data collected locally is utilized exclusively to maintain and enrich your puzzle experience:

  • To instantly restore and resume your puzzle session when you reopen the app.
  • To preserve your achievements, stage completion records, and high scores.
  • To remember your sound and vibration preferences.
  • To optimize grid scaling and dynamic UI measurements across phone, foldable, and tablet screens.
🛡️

3. Data Storage & User Control

All gameplay data is saved in Android's private, encrypted-by-default app storage (Private SharedPreferences). You maintain full control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Reset: You can completely erase your gameplay progress, completed stages, and high scores at any time by navigating to Settings → DATA & PROGRESS → Reset All Game Progress.
  • Device Clear / Uninstall: Uninstalling the app or clearing storage via Android's device Settings permanently removes all local data and saved files from your device.
📢

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep Numora Match accessible to players worldwide and support ongoing development, we partner with industry-standard Google services that strictly adhere to Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Google AdMob: We show banner advertisements at the bottom of the game screen and optional, user-initiated rewarded video ads to grant hints when hints reach zero. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising IDs, and app interaction data to serve ads, combat ad fraud, and measure ad performance in accordance with Google Advertising Policies.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous, aggregated gameplay events (such as stage completions and session length) to help us analyze game difficulty balance and diagnose performance bottlenecks.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Automatically captures anonymized crash logs, stack traces, and device OS versions to rapidly identify and resolve technical issues and ANRs.
Managing Your Advertising Preferences: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID anytime on your Android device by visiting:
Settings → Google → All services → Ads → Reset/Delete advertising ID.
👶

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Numora Match is a wholesome, family-friendly numbers puzzle suitable for puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has provided any personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to delete such data.

🔄

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements, new puzzle modes, or regulatory changes. The latest version will always be accessible directly within the game (Settings → Legal & Policies → Privacy Policy) and via this public document.
